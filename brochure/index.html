About Leaf & Lore

We are a community bookstore rooted in curiosity and care. Our team hand-picks every title, championing diverse voices and timeless classics across fiction, non‑fiction, poetry, art, and children’s literature.

Beyond the shelves, we host weekly storytime, monthly book clubs, and author conversations that bring readers together. When you shop with us, you support a neighborhood gathering place where stories are shared and ideas are welcomed.
